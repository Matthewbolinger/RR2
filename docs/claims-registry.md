## Claim workflow

1. Record exact proposed wording.
2. Attach a source and effective date.
3. Identify claim owner and expiration/review date.
4. Obtain legal, operational, or brand approval where relevant.
5. Change status to approved.
6. Publish from centralized data.
7. Recheck before expiration.

No gated claim should be hardcoded into a page component.
