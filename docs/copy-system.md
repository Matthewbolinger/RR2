# Copy System

## Voice

Human, direct, calm, specific, and operationally credible. Copy should help a homeowner understand the condition, decision, and next step without fear-based urgency or inflated proof.

## Message hierarchy

1. Built Above Standard.
2. Premium roofing and restoration for homeowners who expect more.
3. Clear findings and straightforward options.
4. A disciplined project process.
5. Respect for the property.
6. Verified completion and accountable closeout.

## Preferred constructions

- “Start with the property.”
- “Fix the source—not only the symptom.”
- “After the storm, start with clarity.”
- “Proof should be real—not manufactured.”
- “Ask for the terms—not only the word warranty.”
- “Coverage decisions stay with the carrier.”
- “We take on the claim pressure. You focus on moving forward.”
- “Licensed public-adjusting services are governed by a separate written agreement.”

## Prohibited or gated language

- Best, unmatched, superior, elite, industry-leading
- Top-rated, five-star, review count, project count
- Insured, bonded, warranty-backed, financing available
- Insurance-claim outcome, approval, settlement amount, or coverage promise
- Exact response time, service radius, or years in business

Gated phrases may be enabled only after the claims registry records an approved source and review date. Public-adjusting language must preserve the distinction between licensed claim representation and the carrier’s authority to make coverage decisions.

## CTA system

- Primary: Get a Free Inspection
- Phone: Call Now / Call (224) 500-6825
- Service discovery: Explore Our Services
- Storm: Get Storm Guidance
- Location: Check My Address
- Project proof: Ask About Similar Work

Every CTA should identify a useful next step. The form does not say a message was received until a production endpoint is configured.
