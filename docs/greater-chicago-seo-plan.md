# Greater Chicago SEO and Brand Growth Plan

Last updated: July 30, 2026

## Positioning decision

Raccoon Restoration is positioned as:

> A Barrington-headquartered roofing and restoration company serving the Greater Chicago area.

Barrington remains the verified headquarters and local trust anchor. Greater Chicago is the primary service market. The website does not publish a mass list of ZIP codes or near-duplicate city pages because those pages would be difficult to maintain, weak for customers, and vulnerable to doorway-page quality concerns.

### Gate for future city pages

A city page should be published only when it can include:

1. Confirmed current service availability.
2. At least one approved project, customer story, or locally specific proof element.
3. Unique homeowner guidance relevant to the municipality.
4. A useful internal-link role beyond changing the city name.
5. Approved metadata and a quality review against the doorway-page standard.

## Phase 6 — Local search operations

Status: external setup required

These actions require access to the business accounts and approved operational details:

1. Verify one Google Business Profile with the exact business name, primary category, phone, website, and current hours.
2. If customers are not served at the Barrington address, configure the profile as a service-area business and hide the street address.
3. Add no more than 20 accurate cities or ZIP codes to the profile. Use operational coverage—not keyword volume—to choose them.
4. Add the same current service catalog used on the website.
5. Link the profile to:
   `https://www.raccoonrestoration.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
6. Upload approved project, crew, vehicle, and completed-work photography regularly.
7. Request reviews through the official Google review link and respond to every substantive review.
8. Verify a Google Search Console domain property and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
9. Verify Bing Places and major citation profiles with the same business facts.
10. Review Search Console queries monthly before choosing any new city or article topic.

## Phase 7 — Evidence-led expansion

Status: publishing workflow defined

Priority order:

1. Publish three verified project case studies with municipality, problem, findings, approved scope, materials, challenges, and result.
2. Add approved first-party project photography and source-backed reviews.
3. Build a city page only after local proof meets the gate above.
4. Add one useful resource only when it answers a real customer or Search Console question.
5. Refresh existing content before increasing publishing frequency.

## Measurement scorecard

Track monthly:

- Google Business Profile calls, website visits, messages, and direction requests
- Search Console non-brand clicks, impressions, average position, and indexed pages
- Organic inspection-form starts and successful submissions
- Organic phone clicks
- Conversion rate by landing page and service
- Queries containing service plus city, suburb, “near me,” storm, leak, or repair intent
- Review velocity and response completion
- Core Web Vitals and image-performance regressions

Rankings are a diagnostic, not the only result. Qualified calls and inspection requests are the primary business outcome.
