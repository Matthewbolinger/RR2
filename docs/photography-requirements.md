# Photography Requirements

## Current editorial assets

The build uses four original AI-generated editorial images:

- `public/assets/images/hero-home.jpg`
- `public/assets/images/roofer-inspection.jpg`
- `public/assets/images/storm-assessment.jpg`
- `public/assets/images/gutters-detail.jpg`

They establish layout and mood only. They are not represented as actual projects, employees, or properties and should be replaced before a proof-led public launch.

## Production shot list

Priority 1:

- Real premium completed home, wide hero-safe composition
- Three to six verified completed roofs with consistent exterior angles
- Real inspector on a roof using approved safety practices
- Ground-level storm assessment and photo documentation
- Crew property-protection setup
- Final cleanup and walkthrough

Priority 2:

- Owner/founder portrait and working context
- Team group and individual field portraits
- Branded truck and uniform details
- Gutters, flashing, ventilation, underlayment, and roof-edge details
- Before-and-after pairs from locked camera positions

## Capture standards

- Photograph in landscape and portrait orientations.
- Preserve negative space for responsive headline placement.
- Capture RAW or highest-quality source files.
- Avoid oversaturated HDR, extreme wide-angle distortion, fake storm drama, unsafe poses, and staged thumbs-up scenes.
- Record project ID, municipality, service, material, date, release status, and alt-text notes with every shoot.
- Do not upload homeowner names, addresses, license plates, children, or identifying interiors without explicit release.

## Delivery

Provide originals plus approved crops. The web pipeline should create AVIF/WebP/JPEG variants, explicit dimensions, and mobile-specific widths once final assets are available.
