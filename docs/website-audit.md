## Major findings

1. The empty repository required a greenfield production foundation rather than a refactor.
2. The approved vision board contained a strong identity direction; responsive client-supplied PNG logo lockups were later implemented, while mascot, font, pattern, and photography source files remain incomplete.
3. Most proof claims in the vision boards—insurance, warranty, review rating/count, project count, and financing—were not approved for publication.
4. A wide service architecture was requested, but only Barrington could be used as a verified location without creating thin or misleading local pages.
5. No form delivery, CRM, analytics, call tracking, or deployment target existed.
6. The existing public site’s older headline and insurance/public-adjusting language conflicted with the new brief’s claim-safety rules.

## Decisions

- Build a lightweight static generator with centralized content rather than introduce a framework without evidence.
- Make “Built Above Standard.” the only primary slogan.
- Translate “above standard” into five operational pillars.
- Use generated editorial photography only as visibly disclosed atmospheric media.
- Publish no fictional projects, reviews, ratings, service cities, or operational promises.
- Create all core page systems now while keeping unverified facts gated behind documentation.

## Launch risk

The primary launch risks are operational, not visual: form delivery, approved brand assets, real proof, insurance confirmation, warranty/financing terms, service-area approval, analytics configuration, and legal review.
